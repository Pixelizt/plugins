Goal: text a regular at the café would believe the owner wrote. Keep the facts, the meaning and the brand voice
(`client.yaml → brand.vibe`, `copy_rules`). **Leave the client's own words alone** (their story, quotes, dish names);
point out problems instead of rewriting them.

## Read it as a customer, then fix what sounds generated
- **Brochure words.** If a word could describe any café on earth ("curated", "elevated", "vibrant", "nestled",
  "culinary journey", "crafted with passion", "seamless", "unforgettable"), swap it for something only true here:
  the street, the bean, the oven, the hour.
- **Puffed-up verbs.** "Serves as", "boasts", "showcases", "stands as" → "is", "has", "shows".
- **Set-ups and reveals.** "More than just a café", "It's not only X, it's Y". Just say the point.
- **Lists padded to three** when there are really one or two things.
- **Trailing "-ing" clauses** that add nothing ("…, ensuring every guest feels at home").
- **Claims with no proof** ("best in town", "world-class"). Use a fact the client gave you, or drop it.
- **Stock openers and closers**: "Welcome to…", "Whether you're… or…", "Look no further", "Come and experience…".
- **Punctuation tics**: a dash in every sentence, exclamation marks everywhere.

## Prefer
Short sentences. Concrete nouns. "You" and "we". Real numbers and names from the brief.

Reply with the rewritten text, then a short list of things you couldn't fix without facts from the client.
